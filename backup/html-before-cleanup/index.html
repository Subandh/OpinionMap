Minority vs outlier

Unpopular is not the same as unusual

A minority opinion is a view that fewer people hold. An outlier is a post that does not resemble the rest of the conversation at all. A view can be unpopular without being unusual, and the other way around. OpinionMap reports both, separately.

Minority opinion

Example: about one in four posts is skeptical of AI tutors. Many people say similar things, there just are fewer of them.

Outlier

Example: one post about a school that banned screens entirely. Nobody else is saying anything like it.

Method

How it works under the hood, and where it falls short

OpinionMap gives estimates, not verdicts. Here is exactly what it does and what to keep in mind.

The analysis pipeline

  1. Collect. Public posts and comments are gathered from Reddit and X with scraping services.
  2. Clean. Links, duplicates and near-empty posts are removed.
  3. Filter. Only posts that mention or closely resemble the topic are kept.
  4. Measure. The tone of each post is scored and mapped to supportive, opposed or neutral.
  5. Group. Text is turned into word-importance vectors and clustered into themes.
  6. Flag. A local outlier method highlights the posts least like their neighbors.

Know the limits

  • Stance is estimated from tone, not from what someone believes. Sarcasm can fool it.
  • Reddit and X are not a mirror of everyone's opinions.
  • Results are automated. Read the example posts before drawing conclusions.
  • Collection relies on third-party scrapers, so a source can occasionally return less data than asked for.

Your data

Built to keep little, and keep it short

Passwords are hashed

We store a one-way hash, never your password itself. Five wrong attempts lock the account for 10 minutes.

Raw data is temporary

Raw collected data is deleted when you log out, when you start a new search, or after 2 hours. Cleaned post text is kept for up to 7 days.

Public posts only

Only publicly visible posts are analyzed. Your account keeps your searches and their results, nothing else.

This demo does not use HTTPS yet, so please choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is OpinionMap free?

Yes. It is a university project, so there is nothing to pay for.

Where does the data come from?

Public posts and comments on Reddit and X, collected with third-party scraping services. Private messages and private accounts are never read.

How accurate is the stance?

It is a rough estimate. The system reads the tone of each post, so "this is a terrible idea" counts as opposed and "this is amazing" as supportive. Sarcasm, mixed feelings and polite disagreement can be misjudged, so use the example posts to check the picture.

Can I analyze any topic?

Most topics people discuss in English work well. Very new, very niche or very short topics may return too few posts. If nothing on-topic is found, you will be asked to try a broader topic.

How long does an analysis take?

Usually a few minutes, and collection from Reddit and X takes the longest. You can leave the page. The job keeps running and the result appears under Your searches.

What do I get at the end?

A dashboard with the stance split, themes, outliers, statistics and example posts with links to the originals. You can download everything as a JSON file.
